# Local draft development review

Compared all ten saved local-case-summary-v0.2 drafts in local_llm_evaluation.json with curated.cases on 4 October 2026. Review was performed by the implementing coding agent, not an independent blinded evaluator.

Every saved draft correctly states the supplied open status and overdue amount. Queue descriptions match the corresponding early_stage, mid_stage, late_stage or hardship_specialist value. None asserts employee actions, customer intent, permission to contact or a changed payment arrangement. Repeated source IDs make the presentation untidy but do not add evidence. One draft omits the queue, which is permitted by the narrow summary task.

This sample supports those narrow observations only. It does not establish semantic accuracy for broader summaries, other prompts, hold cases or unseen data. The deterministic brief retains contact gates and proposed action; local generation replaces only summary text. Employee review remains required.
